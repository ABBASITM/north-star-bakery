About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery began as a small neighborhood oven with a simple goal: to bake honest, handmade bread for the people who live and work nearby. Today we are still a community bakery, where commuters, families, and event planners stop in for something fresh and a friendly hello.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

Good bread starts with good ingredients. We choose quality flour, real butter, and seasonal ingredients, and we buy from local suppliers whenever we can. Our dough is given time to ferment slowly, which gives our loaves their flavor and their crackling crust.
